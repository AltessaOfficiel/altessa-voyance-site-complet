How to choose without getting it wrong

If your need is urgent

Favor a simple question or a short written consultation. Avoid mixing love, work and finances into the same message if you want a clear answer.

If the topic is sensitive

Choose a format that leaves room for context. Email psychic readings often let you lay out the facts calmly, without pressure.

If you want to talk

A phone appointment is better suited if you'd rather hear the practitioner's voice and make your request directly.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between an email and a phone psychic reading?

An email reading leaves a written record and suits people who want to phrase their request calmly. The phone creates a more direct exchange, useful if you need a spoken presence.

Do I need to give a lot of information?

Give the context that matters, but keep your question clear. A precise request makes it easier to receive a structured answer.

Does a psychic reading guarantee a result?

No. Altessa presents psychic reading as an intuitive and symbolic form of support. It does not guarantee an event and does not replace professional advice.
